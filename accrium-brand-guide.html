01 / Strategic foundation

Finance that understands the factory floor.

Accrium is moving beyond the idea of a payment rail. It is building a credit layer for capital-intensive supply chains, with robotics as the proving ground. The brand should make complex finance feel tangible, dependable, and in motion.

01
Built around flow

Working capital bridges the moment between a confirmed order and cash collected.

02
Engineered for trust

Onchain infrastructure, expressed with clarity and operational confidence.

03
Made for makers

Ambitious businesses and robotics builders should feel seen, not abstracted into protocol.

Positioning

The credit layer for things being made.

Accrium connects capital to real industrial activity—funding the work that turns an order into a delivered product.

Brand promise

Keep good work moving.

When working capital arrives at the right moment, suppliers get paid, production keeps pace, and businesses can take on the next order with confidence.

02 / Logo system

A route made solid.

The symbol joins a rising path with a stable square: movement held by structure. The open channel suggests liquidity; the terminal block is a point of settlement. A small signal dot marks capital entering the system.

Primary signature / horizontal lockup

Positive / ink on light

Reverse / paper on ink

Clear space

Protect the route.

Keep clear space around the signature equal to the symbol’s signal dot diameter. Keep the icon at least 20 px high in digital use.

Avoid

Keep it precise.

Do not stretch, outline, add shadows, rearrange the symbol, or place the signature over busy photography. Use the one-color mark when color reproduction is limited.

03 / Visual language

Warm precision.

Industrial finance can be exact without feeling cold. A paper-like ground, deep green-black, and restrained signal color create a world that is composed and human.

Graphic device

Follow the funds.

Use fine route lines, waypoints, and small directional breaks to visualize the path from order to supplier to delivery. Keep diagrams factual and legible.

Photography

Real work, considered light.

Show robotic cells, precision components, people inspecting work, and active production. Favor honest materials, natural shadow, and close details over generic crypto imagery.

04 / Voice & messaging

Clear as a well-made part.

Accrium speaks with the confidence of a good operator: direct, grounded, and able to explain the mechanism. It makes finance understandable without flattening the ambition.

“Your next order is ready.
Let’s keep production moving.”

Write with momentum

  • Lead with what changes for the business.
  • Explain the mechanism in plain language.
  • Use active verbs: fund, build, deliver, grow.
  • Be precise about timing, cost, and risk.
  • Keep blockchain in the proof, not the headline.
05 / In application

One system. Many points of motion.

Consistent routes, strong hierarchy, and signal color carry from a funding dashboard to a partner brief or a robotics industry event.
